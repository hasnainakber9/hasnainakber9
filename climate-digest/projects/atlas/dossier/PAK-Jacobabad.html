Jacobabad

Sindh, Pakistan

137,267 women aged 15–49 · 26,710 girls aged 10–14

Of Pakistan's 126 districts, Jacobabad ranks joint 1st for extreme heat (100%); 2nd for flood of 2022 (31%); 7th for flooded land, 2000–2018 (18%); 11th for dark at night (39%). About 186 days a year reach 35 °C or more, and radar saw 42% of the district's land under water in 2022.

Read with care

  • Flooded land, 2000–2018. The record ends in 2018 and misses floods under cloud.
  • Flood of 2022. Where water stood, not everyone the flood affected.
  • Far from care on foot. The source map of health facilities has gaps, so this can be overstated. Check against the district health office's facility list before quoting it.
  • Dark at night. Measures light, not grid connections.
  • Exposed and far. Inherits the gaps in the facility map.
  • People. Population is LandScan 2024; the split by age and sex is from the 2010 census round.
  • Boundaries. geoBoundaries v6.0.0, which may not match the district's current official outline.
