Why winter snow matters for summer

In both regions, El Niño springs tend to bring more snow, and snowier springs are followed by greener summers. This is the likely route behind the link between El Niño and summer greenness in Gilgit-Baltistan.

Data, definitions and caveats
  • Snow: MODIS Terra MOD10A2 v061 maximum snow extent (8-day, 500 m), via Google Earth Engine. Cloud, night and undecided pixels are left out, so cloud mistaken for snow can inflate autumn values.
  • Greenness: MODIS MOD13A3 v061 monthly NDVI, good-quality pixels, over land classed as vegetated in most years (MCD12Q1).
  • El Niño index: NOAA CPC Oceanic Niño Index (ONI), 3-month means of Niño-3.4 sea-surface temperature anomalies.
  • Lakes: JRC Global Surface Water v1.4 yearly history above 3,500 m (NASADEM elevation).
  • Boundaries: geoBoundaries v6.0.0 (Gilgit-Baltistan ADM1, its 10 districts and Chitral ADM2).
  • Normal: the 2001–2025 mean for the same calendar window. Status: Well above normal at +1 standard deviation or more, Above normal at +0.5, and the same thresholds below.
