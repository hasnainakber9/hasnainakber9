In every model, the half of answers with the widest intervals had 1.7 to 2.2 times the error of the half with the narrowest.

The models rank their own uncertainty in the right order. The width is what fails: 1.9 to 7 times too narrow. A fix worth testing needs no prompting: widen each model’s intervals by a factor fitted to it.
