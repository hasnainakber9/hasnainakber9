# Opportunities tracker

Maintained by the Friday run. Each week: add newly found items, re-check deadlines
that are less than 30 days away on the official page, move expired items to **Closed**,
and flag anything due in the next 21 days at the top of the issue.

**How applying works:** Claude finds opportunities, checks eligibility, and drafts
materials in `applications/<slug>/` (statements of purpose, abstracts, CV tailoring,
email drafts). **You submit** each one yourself, because forms need your accounts,
signature, documents and fees. Nothing is submitted on your behalf.
