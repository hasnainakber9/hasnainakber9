# APFSD Youth Forum 2027: submission checklist

**Deadline: 16 November 2026, 11:59 PM Bangkok time** (= 9:59 PM Pakistan time)

Official call: https://arrow.org.my/apfsd-youth-forum-2027-call-for-applications/
Guideline PDF: https://arrow.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/APFSD-Youth-Forum-2027-Abstract-Sumission-Guideline.pdf

## Eligibility (confirm before submitting)
- [ ] **Born on or after 21 February 1997** (age 13–30). ⚠️ Not in the profile; confirm.
- [x] From Asia-Pacific (Pakistan)
- [x] Work relevant to the SDGs under review (5, 3, 13, 10)
- [x] Applicants from marginalised or hard-to-reach places are prioritised: GB/Chitral roots and Young Shapers work strengthen the application

## You must submit TWO forms (both by 16 Nov)
1. [ ] **Abstract form**: https://forms.gle/71NkoUifsRBB5sjBA (needs a Google sign-in)
   - Upload `APFSD2027_Abstract_Akber.pdf`. It has the required title, name and affiliation, and the Intro, Methodology and Findings sections at 100 words or fewer each.
2. [ ] **General application form**: https://forms.gle/5Y5xmRmD7mY7Yf2FA
   - Draft answers are in `application-answers.md`.

## Before submitting, own the work (about 1 hour)
The guideline accepts only research that has been **conducted**. This review was done on 12 Nov 2026 and is fully logged in `methods_log.md` and `evidence_table.csv`. Before submitting:
- [ ] Skim the 4 mountain items (A20, G1, G2, G3), G4 (NCHR "When It Rains") and A10 (Sindh MHM).
- [ ] Confirm the affiliation wording: "Young Shapers" or "Independent researcher".
- [ ] If you want to be listed as the reviewer, make sure you can explain the method in 2 minutes. The limitations are in methods_log.md.

## Notes
- Selected presenters may have to **self-fund** travel. The forum is hybrid (18–20 Feb 2027), and online participation is possible.
- Results come out by December 2026. Unsuccessful applicants are not notified.
- The topic fits ARROW's sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR) mandate and the forum's Day 1 (climate, just transition) and Day 2 (SDGs 3, 5, 10) themes.
