## Seats and funding (call, Table 1)

33 seats: **14 Erasmus Mundus scholarships open to everyone** (Europe and international), 12 self-funded places with a
participation-cost waiver, 3 for the Western Balkans, 4 for NDICI target regions. **Pakistan is not in any listed target
region**, so you compete for the 14 general scholarships (no more than 10 % of scholarships go to one nationality).
Scholarship: €1,400/month. Every selected student, self-funded or not, gets the fee waiver.

### The MS "award pending" issue (important here)

CDE says applicants who **already hold a master's degree** are "encouraged to consider applying as self-funded" (that is,
the fee waiver without the €1,400/month). A master's does not make you ineligible, and a scholarship is not formally excluded.
Your MS is completed, so state it honestly: "programme and exams completed; official award pending" with a KSBL letter.
Your **BS** is the qualifying degree. Decide which category to tick in the form ([[TODO: scholarship or self-funded]]);
self-funded means paying all living costs yourself with no stipend (see https://master-cde.eu/costs-funds/). In the motivation letter, explain why a
second master's: the MS gave analytics, not Earth observation; CDE fills that gap.

## Getting EO/GI evidence before January (choose one or two, all free with certificates; check current dates yourself)

- NASA ARSET online trainings (remote sensing for agriculture/water): https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/arset
- EO College (ESA/DLR-supported MOOCs, e.g. "Echoes in Space", "Cubes & Clouds"): https://eo-college.org
- Google Earth Engine tutorials, plus the ENSO–NDVI code in this repo as a project portfolio
Shortlisted candidates "may be asked to provide evidence of practical experience and completed project work, such as
cartographic or programming projects" before the interview: the `enso-indus-ndvi` project is exactly that.

## Selection timeline (call)

Jan–Feb: eligibility check and evaluation by two committee members (rated Outstanding → Failed) · about 60 shortlisted for
**online interviews in February** · results to applicants **by end of March** · nominations to EACEA by 15 Apr, confirmation by
30 May · acceptance and visa letters in June · year 1 at Salzburg (Austria) from late Sept 2027, year 2 at UBS (France) or UPOL (Czechia).
