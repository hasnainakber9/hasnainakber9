## ⚠️ Urgent: English test (book this week)

- **IELTS Academic** (test-centre version) overall **6.5**, at least **6.0 in every band**, no more than 2 years old.
- The **official certificate must be available by 15 Jan 2027**. Results after the deadline are not accepted.
  Applications without it are incomplete and are not assessed.
- **Not accepted:** IELTS Online, IELTS General Training, IELTS Indicator, IELTS One Skill Retake, PTE Academic
  online, Duolingo, institutional TOEFL, TOEFL MyBest, university "medium of instruction" letters.
- **No exemption for Pakistani degrees.** The exemption list (UK/US/etc. and some European/African countries) does not
  include Pakistan, and a medium-of-instruction letter alone is explicitly not enough.
- Other accepted tests: PTE Academic (test centre) 63 overall, 59 in each; TOEFL iBT (new scale after Jan 2026) 5 overall,
  5 reading/listening/writing, 4.5 speaking; Cambridge C1 Advanced 176 (169 each); LanguageCert Academic (in person) 70 (65 R/L, 70 S/W).
- Timing (typical, **not** from the GEM page; check with the test provider): computer-delivered IELTS results usually take
  a few days, paper-based about two weeks. Aim to **sit the test by mid-December 2026** so a retake is still possible
  before 15 Jan. [[TODO: book IELTS Academic at a British Council or IDP centre in Karachi]]

### The MS "award pending" issue

GEM's entry requirement is the **bachelor's** degree, so the pending MS award does **not** block eligibility.
List the MS in the CV as "MS Business Analytics, KSBL, 2025–2026 (programme and exams completed; degree award
pending)" and upload its transcript under "additional degree certificates and transcripts" with a university letter
confirming completion [[TODO: request a completion letter from KSBL]]. Do not describe the degree as awarded.

## Selection timeline (official)

1. Eligibility review → 2. academic evaluation by partners → 3. **recorded online interview**, invitations normally
   in **February**, interviews **Feb–Mar** (about the top 25 per track; only interviewees remain eligible for the
   Erasmus Mundus scholarship) → 4. ranking → 5. results: admitted with scholarship / provisionally admitted without / not admitted.
- About **17–18 scholarships** for the 2027–2029 intake across four tracks: full tuition waiver, insurance and
  **€1,400/month for up to 24 months**. Self-funded cost for non-EU students: €19,800 tuition + about €30,000 living.
