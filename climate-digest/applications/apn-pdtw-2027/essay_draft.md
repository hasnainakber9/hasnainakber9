I grew up between Gilgit-Baltistan and Chitral, where I founded Young Shapers, a youth network that has run local environmental action with more than 600 young people. My training is in applied econometrics: my MS thesis used fixed-effects and System GMM panels, and I now work in monitoring and evaluation at The Dawood Foundation [[TODO: confirm wording]].

Over the past year I have joined these skills to satellite and climate data. I built a satellite record of this winter's snow cover for every district of Gilgit-Baltistan, and a global district-level atlas of where women and girls live with floods, heat, cold and long journeys to care.

My research interest is how climate hazards turn into losses for mountain and flood-prone households, and who is left out of the data. My preferred subthemes are, first, multihazard climate risk assessment and early warning (subtheme 3) and, second, losses and damages (subtheme 2).

These problems cross borders: the same westerly storms, glacier changes and floods reach Pakistan, India and Nepal. I want to design a regional proposal with peers from those countries. The workshop would give me the proposal-writing and partnership skills I lack, and a team to submit to APN's 2027 call.
