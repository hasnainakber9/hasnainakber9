# APN Proposal Development Training Workshop, South Asia (Kathmandu, 19–23 Mar 2027)

**Deadline: Wed 9 Dec 2026, 23:59 Japan time (7:59 PM Pakistan time).** Shortlisted applicants are selected
**first come, first served**, so submit as early as you can. Official call (checked 20 Nov 2026):
https://www.apn-gcr.org/apnis/public/events/32

**What it is:** a five-day training on writing regional research proposals, for early-career people from
Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka, with mentoring afterwards towards APN's 2027 call
(launch Nov 2027). Theme: climate resilience, adaptation, and losses and damages. Gender-responsive proposals are
explicitly welcome. About 12 participants; APN pays economy airfare, accommodation, meals and local transport,
with at least two funded places per country. No fee. Day 1 is the Second International Conference on Gender and
Technology (ICGT 2027).
