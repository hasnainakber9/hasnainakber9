**Hard deadline: Tuesday 1 December 2026, 13:00 CET** (= 17:00 Pakistan time). The support request goes in
with the abstract, so the abstract must be submitted by then too. (Without support, the abstract
deadline is 12 Jan 2027, 13:00 CET.)

## What the support gives (official page)

- Registration fee waiver, plus a refund of the €50 abstract processing charge (APC) for that abstract.
- Possibly up to **€400** towards travel, at the committee's discretion.
- Decision by **9 January 2027** (support page); the milestones page says outcome **11 Jan 2027**. All applicants are informed.
- EGU General Assembly: **4–9 April 2027, Vienna**.

## Costs you pay up front (not refundable unless support is granted)

- **EGU 2027 membership:** €20 regular, or €10 student (student needs proof of student status). From 1 Dec only
  2027 memberships are sold; until 30 Nov you can choose 2026 or 2027. **Buy the 2027 one.**
- **APC €50** per abstract, card or PayPal only (refunded if support is granted).
- If support is refused, on-site registration would cost €525 (regular member, early rate) or €160
  (undergraduate/master student with proof). Decide now whether you would still go self-funded; if not, you
  can withdraw, but the APC is never refunded on withdrawal.

## Steps (you do these; nothing was submitted for you)

1. **Today–20 Nov:** Rewrite `abstract.md` in your own words (EGU forbids AI-generated substantive abstract text;
   AI language editing is fine). Check each number against

   https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU27/session/60310 . One session only; duplicates are rejected.
3. **Copernicus account:** log in at https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU27/abstractsubmission/ (create one at

4. **EGU 2027 membership:** buy it inside the abstract form, or beforehand at https://www.egu.eu/membership/apply/
   with the **same account**. If you want the €10 student rate, upload proof of student status first
   ([[TODO: are you still a registered KSBL student? If not, use regular €20]]).
5. **Submit the abstract via the chosen session's "submit abstract" link** before **1 Dec 2026, 13:00 CET**:
   title, your name and affiliation ([[TODO: affiliation — The Dawood Foundation or independent researcher]]),
   abstract body, presentation preference (oral/poster), **on-site**, accept licence and rules of conduct.
6. **Apply for Roland Schlich support in the same submission workflow.** The official page says the

   Expect to confirm your early-career status (degree dates) and possibly give a short justification.

9. **Visa (Austria/Schengen):** request an invitation letter by **8 Mar 2027, 13:00 CEST**

## Fallbacks if you miss 1 Dec or support is refused

- EGU27 abstract without support: **12 Jan 2027, 13:00 CET**; present virtually (virtual registration is
  waived for scientists with a permanent affiliation in a low- or lower-middle-income country, and for
  undergraduate/master students with proof).
- EGU EDI Participation Support: deadline **5 Feb 2027, 23:59 CET**, only for caregiving, disability, or
  career-transition cases (https://webforms.copernicus.org/EGU27/edi-participation-support-application/).
- Re-use the same analysis for AOGS2027 (15 Jan 2027) or IUGG 2027 (16 Feb 2027); see `opportunities.md`.
