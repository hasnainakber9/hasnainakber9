# Erasmus Mundus Master in Geospatial Technologies (2027 intake): checklist

**Deadline: Thursday 7 January 2027, 18:00 CET** (= 22:00 Pakistan time), per the programme's Dates page.
Programme: Münster (Germany), NOVA IMS Lisbon (Portugal), Universitat Jaume I (Spain); 18 months from September 2027.
Official pages: https://mastergeotech.info/fees-fundings/erasmus-mundus-scholarships/ and the Dates and Admission pages on
the same site. Checked 27 Nov 2026 (by a helper reading the official pages; open them yourself before applying).

Scholarship: €1,400 a month for 18 months, plus €1,800 per semester towards tuition. People who already hold an
Erasmus Mundus scholarship for this same programme cannot apply.

## Suggested angle (write it in your own words)
- Start from a concrete question you already work on: how El Niño winters reach snow, rain and crops in
  Gilgit-Baltistan and the Indus basin, and who the data leaves out (women and girls in flood-hit villages).
- Show that you already use geospatial data: district panels from CHIRPS and MODIS built in Earth Engine; the
  Mountain Watch snow tracker; the Atlas of the Uncounted map. Link one public page.
- Say what you lack and the programme gives: spatial statistics, GIS engineering, geospatial software development.
- Avoid claiming coursework you have not done.

## Steps, in order
1. This week: ask for referee letters and an MOI certificate; start the 5.5-year CV with documents.
2. Early December: take a short certified EO/GIS course (for example ESA EO College or NASA ARSET) to document GIS skills.
3. By 20 December: draft the motivation letter and record the video.
4. By 5 January: submit (the portal closes 18:00 CET on 7 Jan).
