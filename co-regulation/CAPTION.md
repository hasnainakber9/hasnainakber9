# CO-REGULATION: submission caption

## Submission caption (add #xhopememeprize when posting)

**CO-REGULATION**

Up to 1 in 5 people is neurodivergent: autistic, ADHD, AuDHD and more. Most offices, trains and cities were never designed for their minds.

This film follows one day in 2076, lived by many different minds. They still overload. They still melt down. What changed is the world around them:
- rooms that notice when you've worked six hours straight
- recovery pods for sensory overload
- people who know how to co-regulate, lending a calm nervous system to someone who has lost theirs

None of it comes from nowhere. In 2022 the UK published PAS 6463, its first guide to designing buildings for neurodivergent minds.

The future isn't about curing neurodivergence. It's about building a world soft enough to hold our intensity.
