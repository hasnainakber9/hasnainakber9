# CO-REGULATION: fixed cut

A 1:58 re-cut of the team's draft (`film.mp4`, 2:00). Every shot comes from the draft.

## What was wrong with the draft

- **About nine different men played Leo and eight different women played Maya.** Each 10-second clip was generated separately, with new faces, clothes and rooms.
- **The story never came across.** There was no setup, no explanation of the title, and the ending text never appeared.
- **The sound was out of spec:** mono, 24 kHz, −22 LUFS, true peak −0.3 dB. The background sound changed from clip to clip.

## How it was fixed

**The changing faces became the idea.** The film is now *one day in 2076, lived by many minds*:
- The opening says so ("2076. One day. Many minds.").
- Every scene carries a time stamp, from 08:47 to 23:10.
- The different people read as a montage of one hard day. The room AI's "six hours straight" lands at 14:52, six hours after the 08:47 opening.

**On-screen text carries the story,** in the same trailer-title style as NOBODY'S JOB:

| Time | Card |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | "Up to 1 in 5 people is neurodivergent." / "Most of the world was never built for their minds." / "2076. One day. Many minds." |
| 1:09 | "Co-regulation" / "One calm nervous system helping another find its way back." It explains the title right before the calming scenes. |
| 1:43 | "In 2022, the UK published its first guide to designing buildings for neurodivergent minds." This is the real seed of the future shown (BSI PAS 6463). |
| 1:47 | "The future isn't about curing neurodivergence. / It's about building a world soft enough to hold our intensity." This is the team's own ending line, which the draft never showed. |
| 1:54 | Title: CO-REGULATION |

**The cut follows the story beats, not the 10-second clips.** It runs:
1. the working day
2. the room AI's warning
3. the arrival in tears
4. "I can't do this anymore"
5. the fight
6. the cuff coming off
7. sensory overload
8. "I've lost the frequency"
9. co-regulation ("Look at my shoes")
10. relief ("too many open browser tabs")
11. the golden room ("We did it")

The cuff is slowed to 0.6× for weight.

**Every voice is the draft's own,** separated from its mix and played exactly under its own picture. Lip sync is whatever the draft had; nothing was retimed or replaced.

**New sound:**
- trailer hits on the opening cards
- a nervous pulse and clock through the working day
- a rising heartbeat and dissonant strings through the fight
- an impact when the cuff comes off, then a **sensory-overload wall** (fluorescent hum, tinnitus, crowd, traffic, alarms and clatter) that builds to "I've lost the frequency" and stops dead on the co-regulation card
- one piano note, then a warm pad that swells and settles like slow breathing
- a hopeful F-major theme into the golden room and the end cards

**Picture:**
- upscaled from 720p to 1080p
- the neon toned down slightly for a calmer, more cinematic look
- the cold-to-golden colour arc of the footage is kept

## Known limits

- **Faces still change between scenes.** That is now the film's premise, but a viewer looking for two continuous characters will notice.
- **The look is still neon sci-fi** in the first half, not the warm timber the script asked for.
- **Lip sync is the draft's own.** Where the generator got it wrong, it stays wrong. Most key lines fall on backs or partly hidden faces.
