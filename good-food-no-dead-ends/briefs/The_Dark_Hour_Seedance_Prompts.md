# THE DARK HOUR: Seedance 2.5 prompt pack

Prompts for the 1 Billion Followers AI Film Award (5 to 7 minutes, at least 70% made in Seedance).

The film is 19 clips plus 4 title cards, about 6 minutes 40 seconds in total.

## How to use this pack

1. **Make the references first.**
   - Generate each character portrait in Seedream (section B).
   - Upload the portraits as image references, and attach the right ones to every clip that character appears in.
   - Re-use the same image every time. Never let a clip invent a face.
2. **Lock the voices.**
   - The first time a character speaks well, export 5 to 10 seconds of that voice.
   - Attach it as an **audio reference** in every later clip with that character.
   - This keeps every voice the same from start to end.
3. **Build each clip prompt the same way.**
   - Start with the STYLE block (section A).
   - Then paste the clip prompt.
   - Set the clip length to the duration given.
4. **Dialogue rules.**
   - Speak each line exactly as written, in quotes.
   - The speaker faces camera with their mouth visible for the whole line.
   - One person speaks at a time, with a short pause between lines.
5. **No text in any generated frame.** The 4 title cards are added in the edit.
6. **Generate 2 or 3 takes per clip.** Keep the one where the lips match the words, the face matches the reference, and nothing glitches.

---

## A. STYLE block (paste at the start of every clip)

> Hyperrealistic live-action film, 2039, shot on cinema cameras with real lenses, natural film grain, real motion blur. Grounded, serious, quiet sci-fi drama. Real people with tired skin, pores and ordinary clothes, no make-up look. Real hardware: breakers, keyboards, headsets, monitors. When someone speaks, their face is toward camera and their mouth is clearly visible, and the lips match every word. One person speaks at a time. No readable text or logos on anything, no flags, no holograms, no glowing interfaces, no robots. Cold teal colour before the blackout, warm after the aurora.

## C. The clips

### CLIP 1: THE SUN (12 s), no dialogue

> The Sun in extreme-ultraviolet false colour, gold and orange, filling the frame. On its edge a huge eruption of plasma lifts slowly away into space. Cut: a small boxy satellite with solar panels, alone in black space, suddenly shudders and its panels flex as an invisible wave hits it. Locked-off, slow, enormous. Sound: a single low humming tone, nothing else.

### CLIP 2: THE FORECAST ROOM (20 s), no dialogue

> Night, 2:14 a.m. A small forecast room lit by a wall of gold and blue solar images. **NADIA** [ref] sits alone at a desk with cold coffee. One screen turns red, then another, then the whole wall. She leans in, checks, takes her glasses off, puts them back on. She stands slowly and picks up a desk phone. 50 mm, handheld with a slow drift. Sound: low tone, computer fans, a soft alarm chime.

### CLIP 3: THE CALL (30 s)

> Intercut phone call. **NADIA** [ref] at her desk, phone to her ear, red screens soft behind her. **KWAME** [ref] in bed in a dark bedroom under one bedside lamp, phone to his ear. Each speaks to camera with their mouth clearly visible.
>
> NADIA (low, calm, precise woman's voice, light Norwegian accent): "Kwame. It passed L1 at two-fourteen. Direct hit."
>
> KWAME (deep, warm, slow Ghanaian-accented man's voice, sleepy): "How big?"
>
> NADIA: "Bigger than eighteen fifty-nine."
>
> *Pause. Kwame sits up; the lamp catches his face.*
>
> KWAME: "Say that again."
>
> NADIA: "Bigger than eighteen fifty-nine. You have fifty-one minutes."
>
> End holding on Kwame's face. Sound: phone line texture on the far voice only.

**CARD (edit, 3 s):** IN 1859 IT SET THE TELEGRAPHS ON FIRE.

## D. Before you submit

- **Faces:** every character's face matches their reference in every clip.
- **Voices:** every character's voice is the same in every clip. Use the audio references.
- **Lips:** match the words in every speaking clip. Redo any take that is off.
- **No overlaps:** no two people talk at once, and no voice plays over a card.
- **No readable text** in any generated frame. Cards are added in the edit.
- **Runtime:** about 6:40. Stay between 5:00 and 7:00. Cut clip 8 or clip 4 if over.
- **Specs:** export at 1080p if Seedance gives 720p (upscale), stereo, about −14 LUFS.
- **Rules:** check the contest rules page for the exact deadline, theme wording and the 70% Seedance requirement before you submit.
