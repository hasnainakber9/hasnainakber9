## Judgment calls to make before posting

- **04 Bush whisper:** the photo is from 11 September 2001. The caption never mentions the attack, but some viewers may find the template out of place in a hope-themed prize.
- **05 Asteroid:** keeps a censored swear word from the original.
- **All templates:** they use film, TV and news stills that belong to other people. That is normal for memes, but check the prize rules on third-party images before entering.
