Featured: Five Generations

Zingkha · ep105 · Latest release

How long until women and men are equal?

A girl is born today on the bottom step of a staircase. At the current pace the gender gap closes in 120 years, so the film counts that time in lives: she would be 120, her daughter 92, her granddaughter 64, her great-granddaughter 35. Her great-great-granddaughter would be 7.

This is the first piece in a new illustrated form for the series. The figures are drawn by hand in code, with no generated art. The arithmetic is run in a script that ships with the film, and the numbers come from the World Economic Forum's Global Gender Gap Report 2026 and the UN's World Fertility 2024.

How the work is made

  1. A

    Start from a true number

    Every piece begins with a fact that can be checked: a census, a report, a paper. The sources go in the description, and the script that does the arithmetic is published next to the film.

  2. B

    Direct the AI, shot by shot

    The short films use generative video, directed shot by shot towards one visual idea. The Zingkha pieces go the other way: AI writes the drawing code, so each figure, map and simulation is computed instead of generated.

  3. C

    Render deterministically

    In the Zingkha series each frame is a pure function of time, rendered one at a time in a headless browser and assembled with FFmpeg. The same code always produces the same film.

  4. D

    Finish like a film

    Zingkha narration is a synthetic voice, labelled as one. Its music and sound effects are synthesised for each piece, and captions and thumbnails come out of the same pipeline.

About

I make videos with AI, from the first prompt to the final cut. For short films I direct generative video models shot by shot: storyboard, prompts, edit, sound and grade. For Zingkha, my explainer series, AI writes the animation itself: hand-inked characters, maps and simulations drawn in code, then voiced, scored and cut for YouTube Shorts and long-form. Every piece starts from one real idea, a number from a report or a question people actually ask, and is built to be watched to the end.
