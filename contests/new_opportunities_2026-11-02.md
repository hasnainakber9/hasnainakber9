# New contest opportunities (deep research, 2 Nov 2026)

This list adds to `open_contests_2026-10-29.md` and repeats nothing on it. It was made by searching five areas in parallel, reading each organiser's own page, and having three independent verifiers check each claim. Only claims that survived that check are listed as confirmed.

**The honest result:** only one new contest fits every criterion: free, open to Pakistan, a judged cash prize, and a deadline before 31 March 2027. That is Flick. The rest either have no cash prize, have not opened yet, or have closed. Searches for AI-tool company contests (Runway, Luma, Kling, Suno and others), UN and NGO creative calls, screenplay and design prizes, and Devpost or creator contests found almost nothing that could be verified. That gap reflects what the research could check, not proof that nothing exists.
