All the contests below are open right now and pay at least US$50 in cash. They cover photo, video, AI film, design, illustration and writing. The list is sorted by deadline.

- ✓ = deadline and prize read on the organiser's own page

None of these excludes Pakistan unless the notes say so. Check every contest's rules on AI use and premieres before entering.

## Listing sites to bookmark

1. **Contest Watchers** (contestwatchers.com): the best single feed for film, design, photo and illustration contests. Has a "Free" filter.
2. **Graphic Competitions** (graphiccompetitions.com): free weekly newsletter with a free-to-enter filter.
3. **FilmFreeway** (filmfreeway.com/festivals): most film festivals.
4. **Opportunities Circle** and **Opportunities Corners**: Pakistan and South Asia focus, updated daily.
5. **Opportunity Desk** (opportunitydesk.org): NGO and UN media awards.
6. **aifilmcontests.com**: AI film contests.

Avoid Talenthouse (creators reported going unpaid), Jovoto (domain now spam) and Zooppa (site down).

## Rules to check before entering

- **AI rules go both ways.** iJungle, Animayo, Chicago Latino, Fumetto, Writers/Illustrators of the Future and the photo contests ban generative AI. The AI film contests require it, often with a named tool.
- **Premiere rules.** Busan and Short Shorts require the film not to have screened in their country before, and some festivals reject films already posted online. Check this before posting a film publicly for the meme prize.
- **Payouts.** PayPal can't receive money in Pakistan, so ask about bank wire or Payoneer before entering sponsor contests (AKOOL, PixVerse, Showrunner, TapNow).
